    Take-up & radius (hand bender, EMT)

    SizeODTake-upRadiusGain

    Gain is the one number to check on your own bender. Take-up and OD are standard; radius is derived as take-up − OD/2 and gain as OD + 2R − πR/2, the square corner minus the arc, plus one diameter because stub and tail are measured to the back of the bend. Published gain figures vary by bender, so measure yours the way the handout defines it — bend a 90, then gain = (stub + tail) − the length you started with — and type it in. Editing OD or take-up reseeds radius and gain; editing gain directly overrides it.

    Multipliers, shrinkage and the best-angle table come from the Electrical Construction Course handout; take-up, radius and gain are the common hand-bender EMT values and need checking against your own bender. Check every layout with a tape before you bend.
