    Take-up & radius (hand bender, EMT)

    SizeODTake-upRadiusGain

    These are only the prefills — picking a conduit size copies its take-up and gain into the two input boxes, and whatever is in those boxes is what the math uses. Both vary bender to bender, so type in yours. Radius is derived as take-up − OD/2 and gain as OD + 2R − πR/2 (the square corner minus the arc it replaces, plus one diameter because stub and tail are measured to the back of the bend) — but the handout defines gain as a measured value, so the number to trust is the one you get from bending a 90 and working out (stub + tail) − the length you started with.
